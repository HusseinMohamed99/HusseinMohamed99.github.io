Privacy Policy — StudyFlow

Last updated: 22 September 2026

StudyFlow is a study planner and focus timer published by HSM Software. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. StudyFlow has no account, no sign-in, and no server. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter — your subjects, tasks, study sessions, exams and settings — is stored in a database on your device only. It never leaves the device, because the app does not connect to the internet at all. The released app requests no Android permissions.

Third parties

StudyFlow contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking or software development kits.

Backups and moving to a new phone

StudyFlow does not back your data up to any cloud service, including Google Drive. If you uninstall the app or reset your device, your study data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered by us or by you.

If you use Android's built-in device-to-device transfer when setting up a new phone, your StudyFlow data may be copied across as part of that transfer. That transfer is performed by Android, directly between your two devices, and we have no access to it.

Deleting your data

Open Settings inside the app and choose "Reset all data", or uninstall the app. Either removes everything.

Children

StudyFlow is intended for users aged 13 and over. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the "last updated" date above before releasing that version.
